Product direction

Build the research engine before the social-follow layer.

The expert-following feature is worth adding, but only if OreIQ treats Rick Rule, Don Durrett, Jacques Bonneau, and future names as public-source signal inputs. The product should summarize what credible people are discussing, cite the original source, compare it against filings and technical evidence, and avoid telling users to copy trades or take personal financial advice.

Best framing

Expert Signal Tracker

Add it as a research layer, not an advice layer. Each person gets a profile, public source links, topics followed, companies mentioned, commodities mentioned, and a credibility/context note.

Why it is useful

Good idea, after core ingestion

  • Resource investors already follow personalities and newsletters.
  • It makes OreIQ more mining-specific than generic stock tools.
  • It can surface early attention around companies, commodities, and catalysts.
Guardrails

Do not call it advice

  • Do not scrape or republish paid newsletter content.
  • Do not imply endorsement or affiliation.
  • Do not generate buy/sell instructions from one person's comments.
